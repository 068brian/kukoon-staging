Making sense of the NDIS. Together.

Your NDIS plan should help you live the life you want. Understanding it, finding the right people and getting everything organised can sometimes feel like a lot.

We’re here to help you understand your options, connect with services that suit you and make your supports work together.

Let’s talk about Support Coordination

What is Support Coordination?

Support Coordination is an NDIS-funded support that helps eligible participants understand and use their NDIS plans.

Read more

A Support Coordinator works with you to identify suitable services, connect with providers, coordinate your supports and build your confidence in managing them.

You make the decisions. We help you understand your choices and put them into action.

How can Kukoon help?

Understand your plan

Make sense of your funded supports, goals and how different services can work together.

Find the right providers

Explore options, compare services and connect with providers that suit your needs and preferences.

Get everyone working together

Coordinate appointments, services and communication between the people involved in your support.

Build confidence and independence

Develop the knowledge and skills to make informed choices and manage more of your supports over time.

When life changes, your supports might need to change too.

Moving home, leaving hospital, starting work or finding that a service isn’t meeting your needs can mean rethinking your support arrangements.

Your Support Coordinator can help you explore alternatives, coordinate changes and prepare information for NDIS plan reassessments when needed.

How does it work?

  1. 01

    We get to know you.

    We talk about your goals, what’s working, what’s difficult and what you’d like to change.

  2. 02

    We explore your options.

    Together, we identify services and providers that may suit your needs.

  3. 03

    We help put your supports in place.

    We assist with connections, arrangements and communication between providers.

  4. 04

    We review and adjust.

    We check how your supports are working and help address problems or changing needs.

Your choices come first.

You don’t have to use Kukoon for your other disability support services to enquire about our Support Coordination.

We can help you explore providers outside Kukoon, too. Where Kukoon offers a service you’re considering, we’ll explain that connection so you can make an informed choice.

Who can access Support Coordination?

Support Coordination is available to NDIS participants who have relevant Support Coordination funding in their plan. The type and level of assistance depend on the participant’s individual funding and circumstances.

Not sure what’s included in your plan? We can help you understand whether our service may be suitable.

You don’t have to figure it all out alone.

Whether you’re starting with a new NDIS plan, looking for different providers or trying to get existing supports working better together, let’s have a conversation.
