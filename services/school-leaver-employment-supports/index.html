Your future. Your pace. Your possibilities.

Finishing school is a big step. Finding your way into the workforce doesn’t have to happen all at once.

Kukoon’s School Leaver Employment Supports (SLES) program provides personalised, one-to-one support to help young people with disability develop the skills, confidence and experience needed to prepare for employment.

We combine practical employment preparation, engagement with employers and access to a qualified counsellor to help participants work towards their individual employment goals.

What is SLES?

SLES is an NDIS-funded capacity-building support that helps eligible young people transition from school towards employment.

Read more

The focus is on developing the skills and confidence needed to explore employment opportunities, prepare for work and overcome disability-related barriers to entering the workforce.

At Kukoon, each participant has an individual program shaped around their interests, abilities, support needs and employment goals.

What can you work on with Kukoon?

Discover your interests and strengths

Explore different industries, occupations and workplace environments to understand what you enjoy and where your abilities might be best suited.

Develop practical employment skills

Build communication, teamwork, time management, workplace behaviour, interview skills and other abilities needed to prepare for employment.

Connect with employers and workplaces

We engage with employers to explore suitable workplace opportunities, including workplace visits, work experience and potential employment pathways.

We help participants understand workplace expectations and develop their skills in real working environments, where appropriate opportunities are available.

Access qualified, employment-focused counselling

All Kukoon SLES participants have access to a qualified counsellor who can help them address personal and disability-related barriers to preparing for and participating in employment.

This may include developing workplace confidence, managing work-related stress, navigating professional relationships, communicating needs and responding to challenges at work.

Counselling is focused on supporting employment goals and is provided according to individual needs and applicable funding arrangements.

Prepare for your next step

As skills and confidence develop, we help participants explore suitable employment pathways, engage with employers and identify any additional support needed to transition into work.

Individual support. Real employment goals.

Kukoon doesn’t deliver a standard group program where everyone follows the same activities.

We work individually with participants, combining employment skill development, practical workplace experiences, employer engagement and counselling where appropriate.

Our aim is to help each young person build the capacity and confidence to pursue meaningful employment.

Let’s talk about your future.

Whether you’re preparing to leave school, exploring employment options or supporting a young person through the transition, we’d love to hear from you.
