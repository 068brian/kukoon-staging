Good support starts with the right fit.

Finding the right provider matters. So does having clear information, honest conversations and a straightforward referral process.

Kukoon provides personalised disability support services across Melbourne, with a focus on understanding each participant’s needs, preferences and goals.

We welcome enquiries from support coordinators, allied health professionals, hospital discharge teams, community organisations and other professionals supporting people with disability.

How referrals work

  1. Tell us about the participant

    Share the service being explored, the participant’s general support needs, preferred location and any important timing considerations.

  2. We assess suitability

    We consider the participant’s goals, funding arrangements, location, complexity and the staff or other resources required.

  3. We discuss the next steps

    Where there may be a suitable match, we arrange a conversation with the participant and relevant people involved in their support, with appropriate consent.

  4. We plan before commencing

    If everyone wishes to proceed, the necessary assessments, agreements, planning and staffing arrangements are completed before services begin.

We won’t promise a placement, service commencement date or clinical capability before we’ve established that we can deliver appropriately.

Make a referral

Whether you’re exploring options for the future or trying to arrange support around an upcoming transition, you can contact Kukoon to discuss suitability and next steps.

Services you can refer to Kukoon

Living by Design (ILO and individual SIL)

For NDIS participants exploring an individualised living arrangement, including living with a host or housemate.

We help participants explore suitable arrangements, consider compatibility and develop an approach shaped around their preferences and support needs.

Best suited to: Participants exploring an alternative to conventional supported accommodation who want a more individualised living arrangement.

ILO funding and arrangements are subject to individual circumstances and NDIS requirements.

Refer Living by Design

School Leaver Employment Supports (SLES)

Individual, one-to-one employment preparation for eligible young people transitioning from school towards work.

Our approach includes practical employment skills, engagement with employers and access to a qualified counsellor to help address employment-related barriers.

Best suited to: Eligible school leavers who would benefit from personalised employment preparation and capacity building.

Support Coordination

Practical assistance to understand and use an NDIS plan, explore suitable providers, coordinate services and respond to changing circumstances.

Best suited to: Participants with appropriate Support Coordination funding who need assistance navigating and coordinating their supports.

Participants retain choice and control over their providers, including when Kukoon offers another service.

High Intensity Supports & Nursing

Individualised support for people with complex physical care needs, including high intensity daily personal activities and nursing services where clinically appropriate.

Kukoon’s staffing approach requires relevant qualifications and a minimum of three years’ experience supporting people with complex physical care needs, together with participant-specific training and competency assessment.

Best suited to: Participants whose care requirements call for experienced staff, structured clinical planning and careful assessment of support needs.

Service suitability depends on clinical requirements, staffing capability, funding and applicable provider authorisations.

Where we work

Kukoon is based in Melbourne’s eastern suburbs and supports participants across Melbourne’s east, southeast and CBD.

Some services may be available more broadly across Melbourne, depending on the participant’s needs and service requirements.

We confirm geographic coverage and capacity for each enquiry.

Information that helps us assess a referral

You don’t need to send a lengthy referral pack just to start a conversation.

Initially, it’s helpful to know:

  • Which service is being requested.
  • The participant’s general goals and support needs.
  • Their preferred location or service area.
  • Whether relevant NDIS funding is available or being explored.
  • Any important timeframes or transition dates.
  • Whether complex clinical requirements need to be considered.

Please don’t include detailed medical records or sensitive participant information in an initial enquiry. We can arrange an appropriate way to receive necessary information with consent.
