Talk about ILO

What is ILO?

Individualised Living Options (ILO) is an NDIS home-and-living support pathway for eligible people to explore, design and implement an individual living arrangement. It may include carefully planned host, housemate or other appropriate support arrangements. It does not itself pay ordinary rent, groceries or household living expenses.

What could it look like?

You may explore living with a suitable host, sharing with a compatible housemate or another individual arrangement. A candidate joining our network is not automatically approved or matched with a participant.

How Kukoon helps

We learn about your goals and routines; consider living and supporter options; work through funding, suitability, safeguards, agreements and backup; and support implementation and review where appropriate.

Is ILO right for me?

Your NDIS plan, goals, assessed needs and proposed arrangement all matter. We can discuss the process without guaranteeing approval.
