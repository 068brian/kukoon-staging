Talk about living independently

Your home, your routines

You might want to stay where you are, move into your own place, live alone or share with a person you choose. We start with what matters to you and explore the support that could make it workable.

Support that fits

Depending on your circumstances and NDIS plan, this may involve assistance with daily living, eligible SIL or ILO-related arrangements. These supports have different rules and are not interchangeable.

From idea to a workable plan

We consider your preferences, support needs, relationships, safety, funding and contingency arrangements before any transition.
