Looking for an alternative to a group home?

A vacancy should not decide where you live or who you live with.

Explore your options

Explore different ways of living

You may want your own home, a compatible housemate, a host arrangement or a different mix of paid and informal support. We explore options based on your preferences, support needs and practical circumstances.

What makes an option workable?

Every proposal needs individual assessment of safety, compatibility, staffing, funding, agreements and backup arrangements. We do not advertise a guaranteed home or promise that every alternative will be available.

The Kukoon approach

Living by Design brings together personal discovery, arrangement design and suitable supports. ILO, SIL and daily living funding are distinct pathways, not interchangeable labels.
