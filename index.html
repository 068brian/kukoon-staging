Support should fit your life. Not the other way around.

At Kukoon, we begin with what matters to you: the people in your life, your goals, your routines and the choices you want to make. We explore practical support options with you, explain what may be possible and plan the next steps around your individual circumstances.

How Living by Design works
  • We listen before proposing a solution.
  • We explore more than one pathway where appropriate.
  • We assess suitability, safety and funding before making commitments.

Support for the life you’re building.

Whether you’re thinking about where to live, preparing for work or looking for the right support, we’re here to help you explore your options.
