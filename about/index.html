A life that feels like yours.

We believe a good life looks different for everyone.

It’s shaped by the people around us, the choices we make, the places we feel comfortable and the things that give our days meaning.

Kukoon was founded in Melbourne in 2019 by Filipa Watts, with a simple belief: support should fit into someone’s life, not require them to build their life around support.

Today, we work with people with disability, their families and the professionals involved in their care to explore possibilities, make informed choices and find support that feels right for them.

Established in Melbourne. Here for you.

Kukoon has been part of Melbourne’s disability support community since 2019.

Based in Melbourne’s eastern suburbs, we work with people across the CBD, eastern and southeastern suburbs, with selected services available throughout greater Melbourne.

Our team works flexibly across locations, rather than relying on a traditional office-based model. This allows us to focus on the people we work with and the individual needs of each participant.

Whether you’re planning your next steps, looking for a different living arrangement or exploring new opportunities, we’re here to have a conversation.

Support for different stages of life.

No two people need exactly the same things.

Some are preparing to leave school and enter the workforce. Others are exploring where and with whom they want to live. Some need help navigating their NDIS plan, while others require more specialised support.

Our services include:

  • Living by Design — exploring living arrangements built around the person, their preferences and the people they choose to share their lives with.
  • School Leaver Employment Supports (SLES) — individualised support to build confidence, practical skills and pathways towards employment.
  • Support Coordination — helping people understand their options, connect with services and make the most of their NDIS plan.
  • High Intensity Supports & Nursing — specialised support for people with more complex health and daily living needs.

We take time to understand the person before deciding what support might look like.

How we approach support

  • We listen before proposing a solution.
  • We explore more than one pathway where appropriate.
  • We assess suitability, safety and funding before making commitments.

Meet our founder

Filipa Watts

Kukoon was founded by Filipa Watts in 2019.

At the heart of Kukoon is a belief that people should have genuine choice over how they live, who is part of their lives and what their future looks like.

That means listening before making assumptions, recognising that circumstances change, and approaching support as something personal rather than one-size-fits-all.

It’s a simple philosophy, and one that continues to shape Kukoon today.
