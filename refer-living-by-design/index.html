Refer someone exploring a different way to live

A practical first conversation for support coordinators, OTs, discharge teams, advocates and families.

Discuss a referral

Who we can speak with

People exploring ILO, individual SIL, living alone, alternative household arrangements or daily living supports in their own home. We assess each situation; we do not promise vacancies or funding outcomes.

What to include in your enquiry

Your contact details, role, the person’s general location, the living goal and their permission for us to contact them. Please do not upload sensitive clinical information through the public enquiry form.

What happens next

Kukoon will contact you to discuss the person’s objectives, potential pathway, available information and appropriate next steps. Any service design or suitability decision remains with the Australian service team.
